## 🎯 Pricing Strategy Rationale

### Why These Prices?

**Free Tier ($0)**
- Low-cost customer acquisition
- Lead generation & funnel top
- Build user base quickly
- 10-15% convert to paid in 6-12 months
- Free tier users become advocates

**Comprehensive ($99/month)**
- Affordable for SMB budget (1-2 hours of developer time)
- Covers 50 pages (matches market sweet spot)
- Price point: Industry standard for SaaS tools
- High volume = stable revenue base
- Natural upgrade path for growing customers

**Thorough ($299/month)**
- 3x price = 3x value proposition
- Advanced features justify premium
- Targets growing companies
- 30-40% upgrade rate from Comprehensive
- Lower competition at this price point

**Enterprise (Custom)**
- No fixed price (higher ceiling)
- Allows negotiation
- Land-and-expand strategy
- Services component (implementation, training)
- 10-20x MRR from Enterprise vs Comprehensive
